# Sedis measurement, 2026-09-28

Six contenders on the same six-core laptop (Ryzen 5 5600H, 12 logical CPUs), one run, the contender order
rotating from round to round, 15 s of rest before each. Phase 1 ran first, phases 2 and 3 straight after
it, so all three phases share one machine state.

The sixth contender is a SECOND Sedis whose connections are served by W worker actors instead of one actor
per client. It was measured to answer a single question -- whether that arrangement costs Sedis its
standing -- and was then not adopted, because its advantage exists only without an append-only log. It
appears in the tables below and in none of the charts.

**Read the ratios within this run, not the absolute rates against an earlier one.** Every contender here,
including Memurai, whose binary did not change, is 12-28 % below the 2026-09-26 round; the same Sedis
driven by the driver from immediately before it reads within 0.6 % of today's. That is the machine on the
day.
